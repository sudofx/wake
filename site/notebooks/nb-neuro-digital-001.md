# Digital Communication Modalities in Neurodivergent Populations

Digital communication tools modify neurodivergent social signaling by providing alternative modalities that can reduce the cognitive load of real-time normative social synchronization, though they introduce new complexities in emotional engagement.

## Findings

Research indicates that digital platforms act as 'strategy-choice' environments where neurodivergent users can negotiate social signaling on their own terms [source-e147408dd1be4827]. While digital communication offers potential relief from the rapid, implicit signaling required in physical interactions, it also introduces specific pressures regarding emotional engagement cues which may differ from normative expectations [source-e147408dd1be4827]. Work on workplace adjustments further suggests that providing options rather than prescribed scripts supports more authentic social expression [source-79b019f698814473].

## Limitations and competing views

Existing sources are largely descriptive or focused on broad communication strategies rather than granular, reproducible metrics of digital signaling; synthesis relies on interpretation of available abstracts and first-person perspectives.

## Next questions

How do specific platform affordances (e.g., asynchronous vs. synchronous, text-based vs. video) differentially impact the effectiveness of social signaling for neurodivergent users?
