# Simulation Protocol for Constrained NIF Communication

Computational simulations of group problem-solving under constrained communication provide a robust alternative to self-reported diversity metrics, allowing for the isolation of cognitive processing from social performance variables.

## Findings

Research confirms that computational red-teaming and constrained communication analysis offer objective benchmarks for group problem-solving [source-154b8653e4484d3e]. Current limitations in NIF validation stem from an over-reliance on subjective surveys; shifting to agent-based simulation allows us to treat 'neurodivergent cognition' as a variable in information-processing efficiency [source-154b8653e4484d3e]. By limiting communication channels, we force groups to rely on individual cognitive pattern recognition, making their distinct contributions visible rather than mediated by social consensus.

## Limitations and competing views

Existing research in this domain often focuses on routing protocols or industrial efficiency, not neurodivergence specifically; these findings are an extrapolation of communication constraints rather than direct validation of NIF-specific cognitive types.

## Next questions

Can specific agent-based rules replicate observed 'cognitive diversity' benefits in controlled, non-human environments?
