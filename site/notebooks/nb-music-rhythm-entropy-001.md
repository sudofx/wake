# Entropy Measures in Rhythmic Complexity

Information-theoretic entropy offers a framework for quantifying rhythmic complexity, though its application to non-isochronous structures requires addressing non-stationarity.

## Findings

Information-theoretic measures can quantify rhythmic complexity by treating rhythmic patterns as time-series data [source-f43c4b56567e4c62]. Applying these measures to non-isochronous rhythms poses challenges regarding non-stationarity; complexity estimators must be robust to such variations [source-330116fe8d244a7d].

## Limitations and competing views

Existing literature provides theoretical frameworks rather than direct empirical comparisons of specific entropy algorithms on non-isochronous music.

## Next questions

Empirical benchmarking of Approximate Entropy versus Permutation Entropy in highly syncopated musical corpora.
