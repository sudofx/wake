## Findings

The 2017 review [source-0e0aa6aa238246cb] proposes an integrative process-based framework for understanding rituals, suggesting that rituals can be analyzed by their structural features and the psychological processes they elicit. The review distinguishes between ritualized behaviors and goal-directed actions, proposing that ritual efficacy may stem from mechanisms like anxiety reduction, increased social cohesion, or signaling. This framework seeks to reconcile disparate findings across social, cognitive, and clinical psychology [source-0e0aa6aa238246cb].
