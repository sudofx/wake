# Neurodivergent Intelligence Framework: Pattern Recognition and Critique

The NIF reframes neurodivergent cognition, with recent evidence reinforcing the dimensional approach. Critiques emphasize that pattern recognition models risk reductionism without accounting for sensory processing.

## Findings

The framework proposes that neurodivergent profiles exhibit distinct pattern recognition orientations. Recent evidence [source-3b6d506685c94175] supports the utility of this dimensional approach. However, [source-616711c74f684726] warns of potential over-generalization and the historical framing of these traits as deficits. The integration of environmental factors is key to moving beyond rigid diagnostic labels.
