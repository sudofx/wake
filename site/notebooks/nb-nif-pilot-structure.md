# Baseline Task Structure for NIF Cognitive Diversity Pilot

Refining the pilot structure by shifting to constrained communication paradigms to minimize social confounding.

## Findings

We identified that 'constrained communication' tasks—where group members receive feedback only on collective output rather than individual performance—provide a mechanism to minimize the influence of social signaling [source-429feb1b0f854afa]. This approach suggests we can potentially isolate the specific contribution of NIF-aligned pattern recognition orientations, as standard social adaptation strategies are effectively removed from the variance [source-b83c6629579f49c4]. Previous reliance on self-report surveys [source-35e7097ce5fd4571] is insufficient for validating NIF-based cognitive diversity in team settings, as those measures conflate perceived competence with cognitive architecture.

## Limitations and competing views

These protocols are derived from generic small-group studies [source-429feb1b0f854afa] and may not map perfectly onto high-stakes collaborative environments. The transferability of these task structures to professional work contexts remains a hypothesis.

## Next questions

How do we map specific NIF cognitive types to the variables within these 'constrained communication' tasks to ensure the task design actually engages the intended cognitive diversity?
