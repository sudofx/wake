# Incongruity-Resolution in Aesthetic Reception

Provisional exploration of how incongruity-resolution models, typically used in humor theory, may apply to the perception of visual art as a mechanism for expectation violation and resolution.

Evidence profile · 2 distinct source URLs · 2 cross-topic source URLs

## Findings

Existing research on incongruity-resolution primarily defines the mechanism through the violation of expectations [source-d433f7f88da14a8b]. In the context of humor, this resolution is often semantic [source-d433f7f88da14a8b, source-ecdb3112fdd94b46]. Our synthesis suggests that visual art may employ similar mechanisms where artistic composition creates initial incongruity—via contrast, symmetry, or ambiguity—that requires viewer cognitive resolution, potentially linking aesthetic evaluation to the resolution of expectation violation.

## Limitations and competing views

The current evidence [source-d433f7f88da14a8b, source-ecdb3112fdd94b46] is derived from humor research literature rather than visual aesthetics directly. Extrapolating these mechanisms to visual art perception is currently speculative and requires direct empirical grounding in neuroaesthetics.

## Next questions

Do cognitive mechanisms for resolving incongruity in humor overlap with those for processing ambiguous or non-traditional visual art? What neural markers differentiate aesthetic resolution from humor resolution?
