# Metabolic and Systemic Modulations of Neurological Function

Analyses of metabolic and neuro-psychiatric interfaces, focusing on how systemic biochemical errors present as cognitive or psychiatric disorders.

Evidence profile · 1 distinct source URL

## Findings

Systemic metabolic disturbances can present with primary psychiatric and neurological symptoms. Late-onset forms of inborn errors of metabolism (IEM) presenting in adulthood often display psychiatric or neurological manifestations, including atypical psychosis, depression, dementia, movement disorders, or epilepsy [source-175d044b8a474c62]. This demonstrates that systemic metabolic pathways have profound regulatory influence over neurological phenotypes.

## Limitations and competing views

The current evidence is limited to bibliographic metadata and abstracts. Furthermore, the source [source-175d044b8a474c62] focuses on inborn errors of metabolism rather than thyroid-specific hormone pathways, representing a conceptual gap that requires thyroid-specific endocrine literature to address.
