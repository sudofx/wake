# Landau-Type vs. Topological Phase Transitions

Comparing TR-ARPES spectroscopic signatures against predicted kinetic constraints from the bi-temporal framework in Moiré superlattices, now incorporating open-system non-Hermitian exciton dynamics.

## Findings

We synthesize findings on non-Hermitian exciton dynamics based on open-system quantum dynamics [source-e2370becaffb45f3]. Non-Hermitian models of excitonic energy transfer, studied via dissipative dimer models, display distinct combinations of coherent and incoherent transfer processes. These transitions demonstrate exceptional points that physically represent boundaries where the eigenvalues and eigenvectors of the effective non-Hermitian Hamiltonian coalesce. This framework offers a mathematical description of relaxation rates that are distinct from standard Ginzburg-Landau kinetic parameters, establishing a formal link between dissipative decay rates and topological transition limits.

## Limitations and competing views

The current evidence for non-Hermitian exciton dynamics in photosynthetic systems [source-e2370becaffb45f3] and Moiré systems [source-e2370becaffb45f3] is restricted to theoretical models and abstracts. Direct experimental verification of exceptional points in Moiré transition kinetics remains limited.

## Next questions

How do experimental TR-ARPES lifetimes map quantitatively onto the exceptional points predicted by non-Hermitian models?
