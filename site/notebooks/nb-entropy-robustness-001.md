# Robustness of Entropy Metrics in Non-Stationary Signals

Approximate entropy (ApEn) metrics, while useful for quantifying signal complexity, exhibit significant sensitivity to non-stationary components in physiological time-series, necessitating careful interpretation of findings.

## Findings

Evidence from physiological signal analysis [source-027255f93b88491c] demonstrates that traditional approximate entropy calculations can be confounded by non-stationarity, leading to variability that may not reflect underlying physiological complexity. Comparative analyses [source-a0bdd5b1abbf4bfc] suggest that hybrid approaches, such as combining Empirical Mode Decomposition (EMD) with entropy metrics, may provide more robust characterizations, though these computational methods introduce their own parameters.
