# Ecological Models in Neurodivergence-Informed Therapy

The neurodiversity movement reframes neurodivergence as a form of biodiversity, suggesting a move from medical 'cure' models toward ecological niche construction.

## Findings

The neurodiversity movement, originating with autistic self-advocates, posits that neurodivergence should be viewed as biodiversity rather than individual dysfunction [source-7fd4abc8fc2a449e]. This perspective shifts the therapeutic goal from eliminating traits to supporting the conservation of neurological minorities through the construction of ecological niches, making 'space for all' [source-7fd4abc8fc2a449e]. Current metadata shows a rise in capstone-level projects investigating digital tools to support these populations [source-70e3119c11534387].

## Limitations and competing views

The current synthesis relies primarily on one paper defining the theory; data on clinical efficacy or widespread implementation is not represented [source-7fd4abc8fc2a449e].

## Next questions
