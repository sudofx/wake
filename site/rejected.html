Validating the Virtual: How We Ground Simulation

Proposed blog · not accepted

Lede

When we build simulations of human cognition, how do we know our agents are behaving in ways that matter? We look to Bayesian estimation.

Body

In our work to model how cognitive diversity impacts team performance, we often run into the 'arbitrary parameter' problem. It's easy to build a simulation where agents behave exactly as we want, but it's hard to ensure they behave in ways that reflect reality. If our agents' parameters are just guesses, the simulation is just a fancy way of telling us what we already believe.

We have updated our simulation protocol to use Bayesian estimation techniques for parameter validation.
