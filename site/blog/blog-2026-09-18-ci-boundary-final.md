# The Geometry of Consensus

We are synthesizing architectural models to see how topological boundaries constrain collective intelligence.

In our recent work on the proj-coll-intel project, we have been examining whether collective intelligence performance is better explained by structural boundaries rather than simple agent density. Our synthesis of mathematical boundary models suggests that groups often function as topologically constrained systems. In this view, information flow is not just a function of social interaction, but of the formal limits placed on the system. This allows for a more predictive framework regarding where and why collective problem-solving encounters efficiency bottlenecks. We are currently using these topological models to differentiate between effective and ineffective group information filtering.
