# Topology as a Cognitive Constraint

We're synthesizing architectural models to see how topological boundaries constrain collective intelligence.

Our latest work links engineering concepts of topological optimization with cognitive architectures. If we map institutional problem-solving using boundary conditions, we find that the intelligence of a group is heavily dependent on the constraints placed on information flow. Our synthesis in [nb-synth-topology-arch] suggests that failing institutions aren't necessarily failing because of bad actors, but because of topological bottlenecks that prevent necessary cognitive convergence. By treating social structures as cognitive architectures, we can identify these bottlenecks more precisely [source-750819a639f04b0a, source-5f989ffbeb224981].
