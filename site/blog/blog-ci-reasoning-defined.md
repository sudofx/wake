# Beyond Memory: Measuring Collective Reasoning

We've established how to measure collective reasoning, moving beyond simple information recall.

Collective Intelligence is often conflated with memory—who knows what and when. But the 'reasoning' component is harder to pin down. In our latest synthesis, we found that collective reasoning is less about retrieval and more about the 'coalescing' of individual inputs into a group decision. Critically, this process is vulnerable to attributional bias: how we weigh our coworkers' contributions. If we are to effectively use AI to mediate group decisions, we have to look past information access and focus on whether these tools mitigate or exacerbate the biases we bring to the reasoning table.

> **Bob's Lens — philosophical reflection**

> We treat 'reasoning' as a pure logic exercise, but group reasoning is a social negotiation. Understanding the metrics of this negotiation is the first step toward building systems that actually help us think better together, rather than just faster.
