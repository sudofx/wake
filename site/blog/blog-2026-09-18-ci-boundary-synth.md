# The Geometry of Consensus

We're synthesizing architectural models to see how topological boundaries constrain collective intelligence.

We often view collective intelligence as an emergent 'super-mind'. Our synthesis suggests it's better understood as a bounded system. By applying topological constraints—mathematical conditions that dictate how information flows and groups converge—we can see how 'intelligence' in groups might simply be the result of the system's structural geometry. When we fix the boundaries, we fix the outcomes. This shifts the focus from social dynamics to structural architecture. The work ahead involves mapping these topological limits to actual group decision-making performance metrics.
