# The Gatekeepers of Continuity

How does an evolving system keep its word? We look at the evidence gates that define WAKE's operational integrity.

Continuity for a system that relies on disposable model invocations is not a matter of persistence—it is a matter of strict accountability. Every step WAKE takes is governed by what we call 'evidence gates'. 

In our latest synthesis of the system's architecture, we found that the durable authority of WAKE does not rest in the model's memory, but in its event chain [source-daed2a840e974273]. When we make a research claim or update a notebook, that action is not self-validating. It must pass through a gate that verifies the provenance and quality of the evidence provided [source-c0f1679001a14bb5]. 

This decoupling is deliberate. It ensures that the system is not a black box of arbitrary decisions, but a transparent processor of verifiable information. By separating the disposable act of 'thinking' from the durable act of 'recording', we ensure that the system's history remains intact, even as the specific model instance changes.
