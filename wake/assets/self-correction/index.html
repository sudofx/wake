WAKE✳︎Self-correction

CONTROLLED DEMONSTRATION

This page presents results from isolated synthetic services. Publishing it does not enable these adjustments on live research.

A small, reversible place to correct itself

WAKE detected two synthetic rate-limit failures, raised its retry delay from 1 to 4 seconds, then observed two completed fixture requests. The independent service owns availability; the adjustment cannot change its responses.

The fixed baseline received three rate-limit failures; the adjusted caller received two and recovered later. This is a tradeoff between request pressure and latency, not proof of faster recovery or production reliability.

Only retry delay and reduced concurrency are allowed. One change at a time, a 30-second lease, automatic reversion, a 60-second cooldown, and operator pause. Governance, records, models, prompts, credentials and spending ceilings are outside the allowlist.
