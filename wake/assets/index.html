ABOUT WAKE✳︎

Durable work.
Replaceable intelligence.

WAKE✳︎ is infrastructure for durable, accountable work across interchangeable models, runtimes, and people. Research is the first reference workload.

01

Fresh session

A model arrives without private continuity from the session before it.

02

Durable record

It receives saved objectives, evidence, commitments, rules, and history.

03

Checked proposal

Its suggestions become part of the record only after mechanical validation.

WHAT THIS CAN SHOW

Continuity without pretending.

The record can show what reached a model, what it proposed, what passed the rules, and what changed over time. That makes long-running work inspectable.

See how it works →

WHAT THIS CANNOT SHOW

A receipt is not understanding.

WAKE✳︎ does not claim consciousness, hidden memory, or automatic truth. Sources can be weak. Interpretations can be wrong. The uncertainty stays visible.
