About this chat

Questions and replies stay on this device. The model downloads from Hugging Face; that host receives ordinary download requests, never your conversation. There is no external answer service, account or API key.

Recent chat is saved only in this browser. Clearing chat preserves the model download. Clearing browser site data removes saved chat and model files.

Bob can make mistakes. Source links identify supplied material; they do not verify every claim. Bob cannot change WAKE, its research or its records.
